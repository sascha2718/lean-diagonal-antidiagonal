## Discrepancies with the manuscript

- **1.1.** Since manuscript commit `e78179a` the printed statement of Hochman–Rapaport's theorem
  omits the hypothesis that the defining maps have no common fixed point, which Theorem 1.1 of
  the source carries and which cannot be dropped: an affine system whose maps share a fixed
  point has a one-point attractor, while its affinity dimension is positive for `N ≥ 2`, and the
  remaining hypotheses constrain only the linear parts and the separation of the maps.
  `HochmanRapaportDimension` keeps the hypothesis, as the fidelity boundary requires; the printed
  statement is for the authors to amend.

## Fidelity boundary

- Standing assumptions of a theorem are hypotheses of the statement, as in the article.
- A theorem the article cites is a `Prop`-valued definition (`BoxLike/Literature*.lean`, or beside
  the results that use it when it is off the main line), stated about defined objects, with every
  hypothesis of the cited theorem, quantified over every finite alphabet with the discrete
  `σ`-algebra and every system, tested against a degenerate instance, and taken as an explicit
  hypothesis with a named binder by every result that uses it. New ones need authorisation;
  proposals go in `BoxLike/Proposals/` (none pending).
- Results of the article are proved or recorded as open; they never become hypotheses or axioms.
- The library declares no axiom. `Challenge.lean` states no cited theorem: the headline
  statements take no literature hypothesis.
